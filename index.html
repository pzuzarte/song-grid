    Song Grid help

    Arranges the patterns you've made in the grid editors into a song, and plays them together on your instruments. Song Grid makes no sound itself: it tells each open grid which pattern to play, and when. It needs a current Chrome or Edge. Your songs stay in your browser.

    Visits to this page are counted anonymously with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies.

    Set up

    1. Open the grids you want in the same browser, in other tabs: S-1 Grid, Protein Grid, Bass Station Grid, MPC Grid and TD-3 Grid.
    2. In each, connect its instrument, and tick Sync. The badge at the top of this page shows which grids are linked. Rows for grids that aren't open are greyed.

    Arranging

    • Each column is a section, such as Intro, Verse or Chorus, played left to right. Click its name to change it.
    • Bars sets how long the section lasts.
    • Each row is a grid. Choose the pattern it plays in that section, 1 to 16, or Off for silence. For MPC Grid these are its sequences. A pattern shorter than its section repeats, and a longer one is cut at the section's end.
    • TD-3 Grid edits one pattern at a time, so its row offers Grid pattern, whatever is in TD-3 Grid when the song plays, and the patterns saved in its library, by name. A library pattern plays without changing the grid. Patterns saved in its pattern folder aren't listed: save them to the library to use them in a song.
    • Bars are counted in 4/4. An MPC sequence in another time signature still restarts at each section.
    • Every grid starts its pattern from the first step at the start of each section, so they stay together even when their patterns are different lengths.
    • The buttons above each section move it left or right, copy it, or delete it. Add section adds a copy of the last one at the end.

    Playing

    • Play starts every linked grid at the song's BPM, and Stop stops them. Space does both. Stop on a grid stops them all too.
    • The section playing is highlighted, with the bar above. At the end the grids stop, unless Loop is on.
    • Changing the BPM while it plays changes it on every grid.
    • Under each section, the loop button (∞) makes the section repeat each time it's reached, until you click it again. It turns orange while it's on, and it's saved with the song. You can turn it on or off while the song plays, and every grid changes on the next bar.
    • The up next button (») plays that section when the one playing finishes its run through, even if it's looping. It stays orange until then, and clicking it again cancels it.
    • The bar under the section playing fills as it plays, with the bar it's on beside it. ◀ Section and Section ▶, at the top, jump to the start of the section before or after, on the next bar.
    • Changes to the arrangement while it plays take effect the next time you press Play.
    • While a song plays, the grids switch patterns themselves. Pressing Play on a grid plays it on its own as usual, without the song.

    Assets

    • The Assets panel keeps the patterns and sounds this song uses, with the song, so they're in one place and saved with it, in its backups too.
    • Collect all asks every linked grid for its patterns, and its sound where it has one. Collect beside a grid asks just that one. Collecting again replaces what was kept from that grid.
    • From S-1 Grid, Protein Grid and Bass Station Grid it keeps every pattern with notes or knob movements. From MPC Grid it keeps its sequences, and from TD-3 Grid its grid pattern and its library patterns.
    • Sounds: S-1 Grid's sound controls, Protein Grid's mapped settings, and the sound the Bass Station is playing, read from it. The TD-3 and the MPC have no sound to keep this way: the TD-3's is set by its knobs, and the MPC's samples stay in its project.
    • Patterns the arrangement plays are marked Used. Remove anything the song doesn't need with ×.
    • Load all puts the song's assets back into every linked grid, and Load beside a grid puts back just its own. Use it after opening a song, so each grid has the patterns the song was made with.
    • Each pattern goes back into the slot it came from, replacing what's there. Other slots aren't touched. Undo in the grid brings back what was there. In MPC Grid each sequence is undone separately.
    • MPC Grid puts its sequences back into the open project, by number, on the track being edited. They reach the MPC when you save there. TD-3 Grid puts its grid pattern back in the grid, and adds library patterns its library doesn't have.
    • Sounds are sent straight to the instruments: S-1 Grid's and Protein Grid's controls are set and sent, and the Bass Station's sound is sent through its editor. Undo doesn't bring a sound back, so save one you want to keep first.

    Saving

    • Save keeps the song in this browser under its name, and Save as new keeps a copy. Click a saved song to open it.
    • Songs point to pattern numbers in each grid, so changing a grid's pattern changes the song too.
    • Back up downloads every saved song as a file, and Restore adds songs from one.
